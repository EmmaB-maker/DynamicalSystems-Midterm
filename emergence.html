1. Match the average heading of nearby neighbors.

what you should have seen

When you press play, the fish start out scattered in every direction. In a short amount of time you see them forming small groups (schools of fish). After a longer time (depending on how many fish you start with and add), they form one single school of fish.

Each fish is only following the rules above. No fish knows the shape of the school. No fish is in charge. And yet schools appear.

When you moved your cursor through the school, the fish scattered and then re-formed behind you.

Emergence in this example is the pattern of movement, or the schools that formed.

Self-organization is how they are able to all move toward each other which happens to create a pattern.
