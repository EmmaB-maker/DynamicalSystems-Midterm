what you should have seen

  • Attractors (blue) pull nearby particles toward them. Left alone, the system settles into them. A pendulum settling at the bottom is finding its attractor.
  • Repellers (orange) push nearby particles away. The system doesn't want to stay in that state. They're unstable like balancing a pencil on its tip. Any tiny nudge and the pencil falls (or the particles flee).

Drop particles near a mix of both and watch their behavior. Where do they go? How fast do they move?

NOTE: One function of this code is that it assumes we are looking at only one window of the system. This means that the system goes on forever in all directions and I depicted this by having particles that get pushed past the edge simply leave the map. No walls, no bouncing. Once the particicle leaves this window we assume it goes into another map that we don't have imagined here.
